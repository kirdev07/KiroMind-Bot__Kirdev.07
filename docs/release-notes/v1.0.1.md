## KiroMind Bot 1.0.1

### New
- **App icon.** A chat bubble with the letter K whose upper arm ends in a MindCore node: white on true black, like the app. On Android 13+ the icon can be recolored to match the theme. The bot's notifications use the same mark.
- **Built-in answer base: 30,000 questions on 106 topics.** Greetings, mood, small talk, jokes and facts, tips on films, books and games, and answers about the bot and its developer Kirdev. Each answer has variants for every character: обычный, дружелюбный, вежливый and дерзкий. On update the base is added once, your own answers and edits are not touched, and a backup is made first.
- **Categories and exclusions for answers.** Answers can be grouped, and the base has a filter by category. Exclusion phrases stop an answer from triggering.
- **«Без ответа»:** search, sort by new or frequent, hide several questions at once.
- **Notification** when the bot loses its connection to Telegram.
- **Journal export** from the terminal as plain text.
- **Thumbnails** of photos and videos in the answer editor.
- **«Лицензии» screen** (Настройки → Приложение).

### Improvements
- MindCore understands phrasings like «ну как дела, бот» and «слушай, а кто тебя сделал»: filler words at the edges no longer get in the way.
- The character is chosen in Настройки → Диалоги. «Авто» mixes the обычный, дружелюбный and вежливый styles and never uses «Дерзкий».
- Group mentions and verbose MindCore diagnostics can be turned on and off in settings.
- Accessibility: TalkBack reads switches with their labels.
- Database version 4 (migration 3 → 4 keeps your data).

### Requirements
Android 10 or newer. The update installs over 1.0.0, signed with the same key.
